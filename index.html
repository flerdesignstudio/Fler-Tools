About Fler Tools

Fler Tools is an open-source graphical generative toolkit. We built it to provide an accessible, browser-based alternative for generating unique visual assets while exploring the underlying math and physics.

How to contribute: You can help us improve by adding new tools, fixing bugs, or enhancing documentation on GitHub.

Who we are: We are Fler Design Studio, a team passionate about design and technology.
